## What I use

I get **6–9 pizza tongues** out of this batch, depending on how big I roll them.

### For the dough

- 500 g type 00 flour (13% protein)
- 350 g water
- 5 g sugar
- 11 g salt
- 40 g extra virgin olive oil

### For the topping

- Tomato passata
- Extra virgin olive oil
- Salt
- Sicilian oregano

</section>
<section class="col-md-8" markdown="1">

## How I make them

1. I mix the flour and water in a bowl. I add the sugar, then the salt, and finish with the oil. I knead until the dough feels smooth.
2. I cover the dough and let it rest for 20 minutes at room temperature so it’s easier to roll. I divide it into 6–9 pieces and shape them into balls.
3. I lightly flour the worktop and roll each ball into a long, thin oval. I season the passata with olive oil, salt and Sicilian oregano, then spoon it over the dough all the way to the edges.
4. I preheat the oven to its highest conventional setting (280–300&nbsp;°C, fan off). I bake the pizzette for 10–15 minutes, until they turn crisp.
