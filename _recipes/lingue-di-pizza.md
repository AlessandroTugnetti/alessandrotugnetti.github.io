## What I use

### For the dough

- 500 g type 00 flour (13% protein)
- 350 g water
- 3 g baker’s yeast
- 5 g sugar
- 11 g salt
- 40 g extra virgin olive oil

### For the topping

- Tomato passata
- Extra virgin olive oil
- Salt
- Sicilian oregano

</section>
<section class="col-md-8" markdown="1">

## How I make them

1. I start mixing the flour, water and yeast in a bowl. I add the sugar, then the salt, and finish with the oil. I knead until the dough feels smooth and silky.
2. I let the dough rise for 40 minutes at room temperature. I divide it into balls and give them another 30 minutes at room temperature.
3. I roll each ball into a long, thin oval with a rolling pin. I season the passata with olive oil, salt and Sicilian oregano, then spoon it over the dough all the way to the edges.
4. I preheat the oven to its highest conventional setting (280–300&nbsp;°C, fan off). I bake the pizzette for 10–15 minutes, until they turn crisp.
