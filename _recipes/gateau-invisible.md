I follow [Eryn Folle Cuisine’s original recipe](https://erynfollecuisine.canalblog.com/archives/2008/02/06/7847617.html) for this apple and pear cake. Once I coat the fruit, I can barely see the batter. It does just enough to hold the layers together when I cut a slice.

## What I use

The original batch makes **4–6 servings** in a 20 cm square tin.

### For the fruit layers

- 3 apples, or 4 small ones
- 3 pears, or 4 small ones

### For the batter

- 2 eggs
- 50 g sugar
- 20 g melted butter
- 100 ml milk
- 1 tbsp vanilla flavouring
- 70 g plain flour
- 1 sachet baking powder
- A pinch of salt

### To finish

- Icing sugar, optional

</section>
<section class="col-md-8" markdown="1">

## How I make it

1. I heat the oven to 200&nbsp;°C. I whisk the eggs and sugar until pale and foamy, then whisk in the melted butter, milk and vanilla. I sift the flour, baking powder and salt together and whisk them into the wet mixture a little at a time.
2. I split the batter between two bowls. I peel and core the fruit, then slice it very thinly with a mandoline. As I slice, I toss the apples in one bowl and the pears in the other.
3. I line the tin with baking parchment and spread the apples in first. I add the pears, smooth the top and bake for 35 minutes. I let the cake cool in the tin before cutting it, then dust it with icing sugar if I feel like it.
