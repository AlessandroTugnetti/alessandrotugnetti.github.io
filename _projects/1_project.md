        <h2>Welcome to QFI Alumni & Students Association</h2>
        <p class="lead"><strong>Building bridges between past, present, and future generations of quantitative finance leaders</strong></p>
        <p>Welcome to the QFI Alumni & Students Association – the official community connecting current students and graduates of the Quantitative Finance and Insurance master's program at the University of Turin. Since our program's inception over two decades ago, we've been evolving and adapting to the changing landscape of finance and insurance, maintaining our position as one of Italy's premier quantitative finance programs.</p>
        <p>Our association serves as the vital link between the classroom and the professional world, creating a dynamic ecosystem where knowledge flows freely between students, alumni, and industry professionals. Whether you're just starting your QFI journey or you're an experienced professional looking to give back to the community that shaped your career, you'll find a home here.</p>

        <h2>What Makes QFI Special?</h2>
        <p>The Quantitative Finance and Insurance program has been a cornerstone of excellence in Italian financial education for over 20 years. What started as "Scienze Statistiche e Attuariali" has evolved through various iterations – from "Scienze Assicurative e Attuariali" to "Finance and Insurance" – finally becoming the comprehensive QFI program we know today. This evolution reflects our commitment to staying at the forefront of financial innovation and industry needs.</p>
        <p>Located in Turin, one of Italy's most important industrial and financial centers, our program offers students unique access to both traditional finance and cutting-edge fintech companies. The combination of rigorous academic training and practical industry exposure creates graduates who are equally at home in academic research and real-world problem-solving.</p>

        <h2>Our Mission & Vision</h2>
        <p><strong>Mission:</strong> To foster a vibrant, supportive community that enhances the academic experience of QFI students while building lasting professional relationships that span entire careers. We believe in the power of knowledge sharing, cultural enrichment, and mutual support to create not just successful professionals, but well-rounded individuals who contribute meaningfully to society.</p>
        <p><strong>Vision:</strong> To be the premier alumni network for quantitative finance professionals in Italy, recognized for our commitment to excellence, innovation, and the personal growth of our members. We aim to strengthen the QFI brand both nationally and internationally while maintaining the intimate, family-like atmosphere that makes our community special.</p>
        <p>Our association operates on the fundamental belief that education extends far beyond the classroom. Through cultural events, artistic initiatives, public debates, and academic discussions, we create opportunities for intellectual growth that complement the rigorous quantitative training of the QFI program.</p>
